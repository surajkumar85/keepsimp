KeepSimp PDF editor: third-party components for text recognition and signing
===========================================================================

These run in your browser. They are loaded from this site only when you recognise
text in a scan (Tesseract) or sign with a certificate (PKI.js, ASN1.js; the old ciphers certificate files are
locked with, 3DES and RC2, are KeepSimp's own code, written from FIPS 46-3 and RFC 2268).
